# Narrative

The kitchen, its characters, and every scene. The texts themselves live in `src/i18n/`; their voice: [text-and-language.md](text-and-language.md).

## Setting

One kitchen, seen from the side at the height of the counter. The Cook has gone on holiday and the house is quiet: the lights are off, the tap drips, the fridge hums. Everything the Cook left out — the drying rack full of dishes, the pans on the stove, a jelly in the fridge — has been taken by the roaches and piled into forts.

The Cook never appears on screen, only their traces: a note on the fridge, an apron on a hook, a calendar with the days crossed out to their return. The run ends the day the Cook comes back.

## Characters

### The pantry (the player)

Ingredients past their best, the ones that sit at the back of the shelf. They have nothing to lose. Each is one projectile kind ([gameplay.md › Ingredients](gameplay.md#ingredients)).

| Ingredient | Personality in one line |
| ---------- | ----------------------- |
| **Meatball** | The veteran: round, stubborn, always first in line. |
| **Pea pod** | Three siblings who never agree on where to go, so they split up. |
| **Chili** | Short-tempered; one tap and the spice kicks in. |
| **Cheese wheel** | Old, hard, heavy, and once it starts rolling it doesn't stop. |
| **Popcorn kernel** | Nervous; it's only a matter of time before it pops. |

### The roaches (the targets)

A plague that moves in whenever a kitchen is left alone. They are greedy and vain, and they decorate themselves with what they find.

| Roach | Look |
| ----- | ---- |
| **Roach** | The common one. |
| **Capped roach** | Wears a bottle cap as a helmet. |
| **Colander roach** | Wears a colander: the hardest to crack. |
| **The Queen** | Twice the size, crowned with a jar lid, in the pantry's last fort. |

## Story by place

Each place is a world of levels ([gameplay.md › Structure](gameplay.md#structure)) and adds one ingredient and one material.

| # | Place | The beat | New ingredient | New material |
| - | ----- | -------- | -------------- | ------------ |
| 1 | **The counter** | The pantry wakes up, sees the roaches, and builds the fork slingshot. | Meatball | Glass, wood |
| 2 | **The sink** | The roaches have stacked the dirty dishes into towers. | Pea pod | — |
| 3 | **The stove** | The forts are made of cast-iron pans the meatball can't dent. | Chili | Cast iron |
| 4 | **The fridge** | Cold, and wobbling: a jelly holds up the roaches' walls. | Cheese wheel | Jelly |
| 5 | **The pantry** | The Queen's palace, held together by pressure cookers. | Popcorn kernel | Pressure cooker |

## Endings

One ending, after the Queen falls: the Cook's key turns in the door. The kitchen is a ruin of broken glasses and dented pans, but there isn't a roach left. The ingredients line up on the counter, proud. The Cook's last note reads that the shopping can wait one more day.

## Scenes

| Scene | When | Shows |
| ----- | ---- | ----- |
| Title | On start | The title over the dark kitchen; Play, Language |
| Level select | After Play | The five levels, named after their places, locked or open, hats per level |
| Game | In a level | The level, the HUD, pause |
| Result | Level ends | Cleared or out of ingredients, score, hats; retry, next, levels |
| Ending | After the Queen's level, the first time | The Cook's return, the closing lines, credits |

*After the MVP:* a Place select before the Level select, and an Intro before each place's first level (one illustration, one or two lines). The MVP's Title and Ending are text on the dark kitchen color, without illustrations.
